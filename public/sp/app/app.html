Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Spotify Rewards program pay curators?

Curators earn direct payouts for previewing and rating songs. Payouts accumulate in your verified balance and can be transferred to your PayPal account once you reach the minimum cashout threshold.

How long do PayPal transfers take to process?

Standard PayPal transfers typically settle within 24 to 48 business hours after compliance approval. You'll receive real-time notifications directly on your device when funds are dispatched.

What is the minimum cashout amount?

The minimum cashout threshold is $500.00. Completing your 10 daily evaluations allows you to reach and exceed this threshold rapidly.

Can I evaluate more songs tomorrow?

Yes! Your 10 daily evaluation slots reset every midnight. Returning daily also keeps your active curator streak alive for multiplier bonuses.

Why is identity verification required for withdrawals?

Identity verification guarantees that all evaluations are performed by genuine human listeners, preventing automated farming and preserving the integrity of music industry analytics.
